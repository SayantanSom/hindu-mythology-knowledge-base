# Research plan — v0.1

## Phase 1: Research foundations

- Draft a non-exclusive taxonomy of sacred literature (Shruti, Smriti, Vedic auxiliary texts, Itihasa, Purana, Agama, Tantra, devotional and regional corpora, commentaries).
- Approve the bibliographic, textual, and citation metadata model.
- Establish editorial statuses: proposed, catalogued, in_research, reviewed, published.
- Create a controlled vocabulary of language and script identifiers.

## Phase 2: Master catalogue (broad, multilingual)

- Create a record for each distinct **work** without assuming every title refers to one stable text.
- Link recensions, manuscripts, editions, translations, and commentaries as distinct records.
- Attach one or more categories per work with attribution to a classification system or source.
- Track whether metadata is sourced, provisional, or awaiting review.

## Phase 3: Focused research (Sanskrit first)

Research the four Vedas and their textual strata, principal Upanishads, Ramayana, Mahabharata, and the conventionally enumerated 18 Mahapuranas. Record alternate lists and recension differences rather than forcing one list.

### First deity research sample: 33 deities

Begin from Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 3.9 and compare relevant Vedic and later enumerations. Keep named lists source-specific. Track the meaning and history of 'koti' as a separate research question: the 33-crore interpretation does not itself supply 330 million independently attested names.

## Phase 4: Public web experience

- Browse scriptures and categories.
- View verified source passages and translations.
- Explore deity entries, stories, and concepts.
- Compare source-specific claims.

## Phase 5: Editorial expansion

Roles: contributor, reviewer, editor, administrator; review history, annotations, and provenance.

## Initial acceptance criteria

- A scripture can belong to multiple sourced categories.
- A work has distinct recensions/editions/translations.
- A claim can be linked to one or more precise passages and framed as a tradition-specific interpretation.
- Draft or unverified material is never displayed as verified.
