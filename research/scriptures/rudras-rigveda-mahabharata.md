# Rudra research dossier — Ṛgveda, Bṛhadāraṇyaka, Mahābhārata

## Method

Keep distinct (a) hymns addressed to Rudra, (b) a philosophical explanation of the eleven Rudras, and (c) an epic genealogy of eleven named Rudras. Do not claim that the Ṛgvedic hymns enumerate the Mahābhārata's eleven names. Passage locations and working translations are recorded; recensions and edition alignment remain editorial tasks.

## Ṛgveda

- **2.33**: Rudra addressed as father of the Maruts; invoked for remedies, long life and relief from harm; formidable arrows and benevolence both matter. Sanskrit: https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_veda/rudrasUktambasic.html . Secondary scholarly orientation: Jamison and Brereton, *The Rigveda* (Oxford, 2014), hymn 2.33 commentary (accessible excerpt). Attribution in that source: Gṛtsamada.
- **1.114**: prayer for Rudra's favor and protection of family, people and cattle, against the danger of his weapons. Same Sanskrit compilation.
- **7.46**: prayer to formidable Rudra for protection and well-being. Same Sanskrit compilation.
- These are *hymns to Rudra*, not independently verified eleven-member genealogies. The three accounts are marked for translation/edition-context review.

## Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 3.9.4

In the Śākalya–Yājñavalkya dialogue, ten prāṇas and Ātman are identified as eleven. Departure from the body makes people weep, explaining the name. The Sanskrit does not separately enumerate five perception faculties and five action faculties. Working Sanskrit/Max Müller English: https://upanishads.org.in/u/brihadaranyaka/p/3.9.4 . Another translator (Madhavananda) renders the eleventh differently; preserve translation distinction: https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/BRU/3.9.4 .

## Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva, Sambhava Parva, Ganguli section LXVI

**Narrator:** Vaiśampāyana (within epic frame); **genealogical context:** origins and descendants of Brahmā's mind-born sages. Sthāṇu has eleven sons identified as Rudras.

Names as normalized against the Sanskrit verse display and Ganguli English:
1. Mṛgavyādha
2. Sarpa
3. Nirṛti
4. Ajaikapād
5. Ahirbudhnya
6. Pinākin
7. Dahana
8. Īśvara
9. Kapālin
10. Sthāṇu
11. Bharga

**Editorial caveats:** Ganguli's English spellings vary (Ahivradhna, etc.). Sthāṇu appears both as progenitor and in the list. This may be a genuine textual complication, not a typo to auto-correct. Verse/chapter numbers from an online Sanskrit presentation and Ganguli section LXVI are not guaranteed to match a critical edition. Avoid attributing the speech directly to Vyāsa, or claiming the epic list is the original Ṛgvedic meaning.
