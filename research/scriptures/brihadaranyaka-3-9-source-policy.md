## Preliminary finding

The preferred page's passage 3.9.2 enumerates eight Vasus, eleven Rudras, twelve Ādityas, Indra, and Prajāpati, totaling thirty-three. This is **a page-attested preliminary finding**, not a fully edition-verified critical reading.

## Editorial policy

Source priority and verification are separate attributes. Keep secondary references, conflicting readings, and uncertainty visible. Do not populate an edition record with invented editor/publisher metadata or assert a recension without proof.
